Pauli-X flips |0> to |1> and |1> to |0>. On the sphere it reflects the vector around the X axis.
Hadamard turns a definite state into an equal superposition. It moves |0> to the +X direction.
Pauli-Z keeps |0> unchanged and flips the phase of |1>. It rotates the vector 180 degrees around Z.
Phase, or pi-style, gate adds a chosen angle to the |1> part of the state. Pick the angle above before pressing P.
Identity leaves the qubit exactly as it is. It is useful as a no-change reference gate.
